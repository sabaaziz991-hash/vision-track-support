Common Questions

  • Free access includes Eye Flow.
  • Vision Plus unlocks the full routine library, multiple progress profiles, CSV export, and the routine builder.
  • Blink Garden camera awareness is optional and can be left off.
  • Purchases and refunds are managed through Apple.

Subscriptions

Monthly and annual Vision Plus plans renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings.
