Contact

ViewWell Support Issues

Use this channel for privacy questions, subscription questions, app behavior issues, and device compatibility reports. A GitHub sign-in may be required to open a new issue.

GitHub Issues are public, so do not include personal, medical, payment, or sensitive information. For Apple ID billing, subscription cancellation, or refund requests, use Apple billing support.

No-Login Help

You can read this support page, the privacy policy, terms, and existing public GitHub issues without creating a ViewWell account. ViewWell does not require an app account or sign-in.

When opening a support issue, include the app version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of the question or issue.

Common Questions

  • Free access includes Eye Flow.
  • Vision Plus unlocks the full routine library, multiple progress profiles, CSV export, and the routine builder.
  • Blink Garden camera awareness is optional and can be left off.
  • Purchases and refunds are managed through Apple.

Subscriptions

Monthly and annual Vision Plus plans renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID subscription settings.
