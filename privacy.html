Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

ViewWell provides consumer wellness routine games for screen comfort, visual rest, blink habits, and visual task practice. This policy explains what the app stores and how optional camera awareness works.

Information Stored In The App

  • Routine completions, selected routine durations, comfort check-ins, saved profile names, and saved wellness routine plans may be stored locally on the device.
  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not collect contact information in the app.
  • The app does not use HealthKit.
  • The app does not include ads, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Optional Camera Awareness

Blink Garden includes optional front-camera blink awareness. Camera input is processed on device for the routine experience. Photos and video are not stored by the app, uploaded, or shared. Camera access can stay off, and the routine remains usable in manual mode.

Purchases

Vision Plus purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives purchase entitlement status for Vision Plus access. Payment details are managed by Apple, not by ViewWell.

Data Sharing

ViewWell does not sell personal data. The current app does not transmit routine progress, camera data, or profile data to a developer server.

Support Requests

Support requests use public GitHub Issues from the ViewWell support page. GitHub sign-in may be required to open an issue. Do not include personal, medical, payment, or sensitive information in a public support issue.

Public support posts are hosted by GitHub and are not collected inside the ViewWell app. For Apple ID billing, subscription cancellation, or refund requests, use Apple billing support.

Retention, Deletion, And Consent

Routine progress, comfort check-ins, profile names, and saved routine plans remain local on the device until you delete the app or remove the app's local data through iOS. ViewWell does not keep a developer-server copy of that local app data.

You can revoke camera permission at any time in iOS Settings. If camera access is off, Blink Garden stays available in manual mode.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other data features. Review this page for the latest policy before each release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the ViewWell support page.
