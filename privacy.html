Vision Track

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

Vision Track provides consumer wellness routine games for screen comfort, visual rest, blink habits, and visual task practice. This policy explains what the app stores and how optional camera awareness works.

Information Stored In The App

  • Routine completions, selected routine durations, comfort check-ins, saved profile names, and saved wellness routine plans may be stored locally on the device.
  • The app does not require an account.
  • The app does not collect contact information in the app.
  • The app does not use HealthKit.
  • The app does not include ads, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Optional Camera Awareness

Blink Garden includes optional front-camera blink awareness. Camera input is processed on device for the routine experience. Photos and video are not stored by the app, uploaded, or shared. Camera access can stay off, and the routine remains usable in manual mode.

Purchases

Vision Plus purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives purchase entitlement status for Vision Plus access. Payment details are managed by Apple, not by Vision Track.

Data Sharing

Vision Track does not sell personal data. The current app does not transmit routine progress, camera data, or profile data to a developer server.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or other data features. Review this page for the latest policy before each release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Vision Track support page.
